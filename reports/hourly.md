## Uncertainty and the capacity decision

80% intervals (quantile gradient boosting): coverage **78.9%** (binomial p = 0.066), 10.6% below / 10.5% above, mean width 92. Conformal calibration on the last 8 development weeks widened them to 98 and over-covered (82.7%), so the plain quantile intervals are used.

Capacity plan: unmet demand costs 3× idle capacity, so the cost-minimising plan is the 75% quantile. Baseline plans get a buffer tuned on development data for the same cost.
